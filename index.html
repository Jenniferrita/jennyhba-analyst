02

Community Pharmacy Delivery System: from a 7-day existing system, to a 3 days improvement plan, also indentified an error-prone delivery process

ARMDARI. Health-tech Business Analyst, August 2026 till date.

The problem
Routine home delivery took up to 7 days against a 3-day target. About 12% of orders had capture errors, staff could see the status of only 40% of orders, and patients kept calling to ask where their order was.
My role
Business analyst on an integrated project covering process mapping, root-cause and gap analysis, and the business requirements document. The brief was to explain why the gaps exist before proposing a fix.
What I did
Mapped the current (AS-IS) and proposed (TO-BE) processes. Built a fishbone across six cause categories, ranked eight priority causes and gave each a validation check. Ran an eight-gap analysis with actions, owners and priorities. Wrote the requirements document: analysis of eight stakeholder groups, 33 business, stakeholder, functional and non-functional requirements prioritized with MoSCoW, 8 acceptance criteria, and a traceability matrix linking every gap to a requirement and a test.
What it produced
A single report taking the problem from current process to future process: one validated order record, one sequenced pharmacy and stock check, scheduled delivery, milestone patient updates and a pre-dispatch accuracy check, with pharmacy approval kept as a human decision. It includes a 12-week roadmap, risks, governance and sign-off. The working targets (errors under 1%, status visibility 100%, delivery in 3 days by week 12) are proposed for stakeholder confirmation, not claimed as results.

03

Project TRACE: a business case for safer specimen identification and laboratory tracking

ARMDARI. Health-tech Business Analyst, August 2026 till date. Hospital laboratory specimen tracking.

The problem
A community hospital needed a justified case for tracking lab specimens with barcodes instead of manual handling.
My role
Business analyst for the stakeholder analysis, the business case and the SDLC and validation plan.
What I did
Documented root causes and the current-state process, set objectives, scope, assumptions, constraints and risks, and compared three options on feasibility, cost, benefit and risk. Built a stakeholder register, power-interest grid, RACI matrix and communication plan, and planned the SDLC and validation approach.
What it produced
A complete business case document and a 14-slide executive deck presenting the recommended option, its stakeholders and its delivery plan.
  • Business case
  • Stakeholder analysis
  • RACI
  • Options analysis
  • SDLC and validation
